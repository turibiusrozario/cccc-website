Cross Campus Climate Coalition

We are a network of Massachusetts higher education individuals and their organizations working to share resources, events, and opportunities to maximize youth influence on climate and sustainability progress in our state.

In a world filled with polycrisis –

WE AIM to bring the concerns and voices of the youth together, helping them find each other, belong, and make a difference. In doing so, we seek to enable coordinated collaboration, sustainable and tangible change, and to persevere together against our generation’s most precarious problems.

WE BELIEVE young people have the power to channel climate anxiety into meaningful, local climate actions, in ways that older adults may not be able to.

WE WORK towards this by forming connections (with student liaisons, campus staff, non-profit partners, industry, as well as local government representatives across different campuses and age groups), fostering intergenerational collaboration, creating network maps, hosting cross-campus mixers, and sharing resources and events.

The biggest asset of any group is its people! Although we have shared objectives and methods, we welcome your opinion and enthusiasm and would like to support your endeavours as well.
