Cross Campus Climate Coalition

We are a network of Massachusetts higher education individuals and their organizations. We share resources, events, and opportunities. Our goal is to give young people more influence on climate and sustainability progress in our state.

What we are

We use the short name CCCC for Cross Campus Climate Coalition. We live in a time of polycrisis, which means several serious problems are happening at the same time. These are our three main points:

What we aim to do

We bring the concerns and voices of young people together. We help them find each other, belong, and make a difference. We want to enable coordinated collaboration and sustainable, tangible change. We want to keep going together against the most difficult problems of our generation.

What we believe

Young people have the power to turn climate anxiety into meaningful, local climate action. They can do this in ways that older adults may not be able to.

How we work

We form connections with student liaisons, campus staff, non-profit partners, industry, and local government representatives. They come from different campuses and age groups. We also support collaboration between age groups, create network maps, host cross-campus mixers, and share resources and events.

People are our biggest asset. We share objectives and methods, and we also welcome your opinions and enthusiasm. We would like to support your projects too.
